Dentist for Herston

Dental Care for Busy Shifts, Study and Family Life

With the Royal Brisbane and Women's Hospital precinct and university campuses nearby, Herston is full of people working long hours and studying hard. Precision Dental, a short trip away in Fortitude Valley, offers dental care that respects your time and helps you feel looked after.

Book Online Call (07) 3852 1160

About Precision Dental

People who spend their days caring for others often put their own health last. We make it easy to change that, with prompt bookings, efficient appointments and a team who treat you with the same attention you give your own patients, students or family.

Our coordinators can arrange a series of appointments ahead of time, so your care fits around rosters and exam periods. Learn more About Precision Dental and what guides our work.

Our Dental Services

Grinding and Clenching

Stress, irregular sleep and long shifts can contribute to grinding and clenching. We check for wear, cracks and jaw tension, and can make a custom night splint to help protect your teeth.

Explore Grinding and Clenching

Care for Anxious Patients

Our care for anxious patients offers extra time, clear explanations and the option of happy gas, so treatment feels manageable even when you are tired or stressed.

Explore Care for Anxious Patients

General Dentistry

From regular cleans to root canal therapy and extractions, our General Dentistry services cover the everyday care that keeps problems at bay.

Explore General Dentistry

Emergency and Cosmetic Care

Our Emergency Dentistry team sees urgent pain, swelling and breakages, while our Cosmetic Dentistry options include whitening, veneers and clear aligners.

Why Herston Patients Choose Precision Dental

Honesty

We give clear, evidence-informed explanations that health professionals and students appreciate, and we are always happy to answer detailed questions.

Comfort

We aim to keep waiting to a minimum and let you set the pace of each appointment.

Quality

Tools such as an intra-oral camera, digital X-rays and rotary endodontic instruments support accurate, efficient treatment.

Convenience

An 8am start on Fridays and 7pm finish on Tuesdays can work around many rosters and class timetables.

Safety

Our infection prevention and control protocols follow Dental Board of Australia guidelines and are reviewed as part of our accreditation.

Special Offers and Patient Benefits

Families with eligible children can use the Child Dental Benefits Schedule, and we welcome Department of Veterans' Affairs card holders. Please contact us to confirm your entitlements before your visit.
