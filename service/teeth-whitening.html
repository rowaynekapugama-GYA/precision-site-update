Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth Whitening in Fortitude Valley

Over time, teeth can darken or pick up stains from coffee, tea, red wine and other everyday habits. Professional teeth whitening, provided and supervised by a dental team, can lighten the natural colour of your teeth and help reduce surface discolouration.

At Precision Dental, we begin by making sure your teeth and gums are healthy and that whitening is suitable for you, then recommend an approach that suits your needs.

What Is Professional Teeth Whitening?

Professional teeth whitening uses whitening gels that are applied to the teeth under dental supervision. The gel breaks down stain molecules within the enamel and dentine, gradually lightening the tooth colour.

Depending on your needs, whitening may be performed with custom-made take-home trays, in the chair at our practice, or with a combination of both. Unlike many over-the-counter products, professional whitening is tailored to your teeth, and your dental team monitors your gums and sensitivity throughout.

Whitening works on natural teeth only. Fillings, crowns, veneers and bonding will not change colour, so we will discuss how existing restorations may look after whitening.

Is Teeth Whitening Right for You?

Whitening may be suitable for adults with:

  • Yellowing or darkening of teeth with age
  • Surface stains from food, drinks or smoking
  • An upcoming occasion where they would like a brighter smile
  • Plans for veneers or crowns, where whitening first helps set the shade

Whitening may not be suitable if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have untreated decay or gum disease, or have significant tooth sensitivity. Some types of internal discolouration, such as those caused by certain medications or trauma, may respond less predictably, and alternatives such as composite resin veneers or porcelain veneers may be discussed.

What Happens at Your Appointment

Assessment

We start with an examination to check for decay, gum inflammation, worn enamel and existing restorations. A professional clean is often recommended beforehand so the whitening gel can make even contact with the tooth surface. We record your starting shade so you can compare progress.

Take-Home Whitening

We take a digital scan or impressions to create trays that fit your teeth closely. You will receive your trays with professional whitening gel and clear instructions on how long to wear them each day.

In-Chair Whitening

For in-chair whitening, your gums and lips are carefully protected, and whitening gel is applied to your teeth for a set time under close supervision. Take-home trays may be provided to continue or maintain your results.

Aftercare and Ongoing Care

Some temporary sensitivity is common during and shortly after whitening. A sensitive toothpaste can help. For the first 48 hours, limit strongly coloured foods and drinks. Whitening results are not permanent, and teeth may gradually darken again. Occasional top-up treatments with your trays, along with regular check-ups and cleans, can help maintain your shade.

Why Choose Precision Dental for Teeth Whitening

Whitening tends to be most predictable when it is planned around your oral health. We check for underlying concerns first, tailor the approach to your teeth and sensitivity, and give honest guidance about what whitening can and cannot change.

Fees, Health Funds and Payment Options

The cost of your treatment depends on your individual needs. After your examination, we talk you through your options and the expected fees before any treatment begins. We accept most major health funds, process claims on the spot through HICAPS and are a preferred provider for Bupa, HCF and CBHS. Interest-free payment plans are available through DentiCare for eligible patients, subject to terms and conditions. You can learn more on our Prices and Payment Options page.
