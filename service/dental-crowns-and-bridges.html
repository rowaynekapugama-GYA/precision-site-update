Restorative Dentistry

Dental Crowns and Bridges in Fortitude Valley

Teeth that are cracked, heavily filled or badly worn can become fragile and uncomfortable. Missing teeth can affect how you chew, how your remaining teeth are positioned and how you feel when you smile. Dental crowns and bridges are fixed restorations that help protect weakened teeth and replace missing ones.

At Precision Dental, we explain every suitable option, including when a smaller restoration or an alternative such as an implant may be more appropriate.

What Are Dental Crowns and Bridges?

A dental crown is a custom-made cap that covers the entire visible part of a tooth. It restores the tooth's shape and strength and helps protect it from further damage. Crowns can be made from ceramic, porcelain fused to metal, zirconia or gold, depending on the tooth and your needs.

A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth. It typically consists of an artificial tooth held in place by crowns on the neighbouring teeth, or on dental implants. Once fitted, a bridge is fixed in place and is not removed for cleaning.

Are Crowns and Bridges Right for You?

A crown may be recommended if you have:

  • A tooth with a large filling and little remaining structure
  • A cracked or fractured tooth
  • A tooth that has had root canal therapy
  • Severely worn or discoloured teeth

A bridge may be considered if you have one or more missing teeth and healthy neighbouring teeth or implants to support it. Because a traditional bridge involves shaping the adjacent teeth, we will also discuss alternatives such as dental implants and dentures, along with the benefits and limitations of each.

What Happens at Your Appointment

Assessment and Planning

We examine your teeth, take digital X-rays and check your bite and gum health. We explain our findings, outline your options and provide a written quote.

Preparation

Using local anaesthetic, the tooth or teeth are carefully shaped to make space for the restoration. A digital scan records the prepared teeth in detail.

Your Restoration

Many single crowns can be designed and milled in our practice using CEREC, allowing same-day fitting. Other crowns and most bridges are made by a dental laboratory, in which case a temporary restoration is placed while you wait.

Fitting

We check the fit, appearance and bite of your restoration, make any refinements, and cement or bond it securely in place.

Aftercare and Ongoing Care

Brush twice daily and clean between your teeth each day. For bridges, floss threaders, interdental brushes or a water flosser help clean beneath the replacement tooth. Avoid chewing very hard objects, and wear a night splint if you grind. Regular check-ups and cleans allow us to monitor the edges of your restorations and the health of your gums.

Why Choose Precision Dental for Crowns and Bridges

We combine careful diagnosis with digital scanning and in-house CEREC technology, which means many single crowns can be completed in one visit. Dr Billy Choi takes time to explain every option, so you understand why a crown or bridge is recommended and what alternatives are available.

Fees, Health Funds and Payment Options

We accept most major health funds, process claims on the spot through HICAPS and are a preferred provider for Bupa, HCF and CBHS. Interest-free payment plans are available through DentiCare for eligible patients, subject to terms and conditions. You can learn more on our Prices and Payment Options page.
