Restorative Dentistry

CEREC Same-Day Crowns in Fortitude Valley

When a tooth is cracked, heavily filled or weakened after root canal therapy, a crown can help protect it and restore its function. Traditionally, crowns required two appointments, a temporary crown and a wait of a week or more while a laboratory made your restoration. With CEREC, many crowns can be designed, made and fitted at Precision Dental in a single visit.

We use CEREC because it offers a precise, efficient way to restore teeth while reducing the number of appointments many patients need.

What Is CEREC?

CEREC is a digital system that allows ceramic restorations to be created in the dental practice. Instead of traditional impression putty, a 3D intra-oral scanner captures a detailed digital model of your tooth. Your crown is then designed on screen and milled from a solid block of high-strength ceramic in our practice.

CEREC can be used to make crowns, inlays, onlays and some veneers. Because the process takes place on site, your dentist can oversee each step, from design to final fit.

Is a CEREC Crown Right for You?

A CEREC restoration may be suitable if you have:

  • A cracked or broken tooth
  • A tooth with a large, failing filling
  • A tooth that needs protection after root canal therapy
  • A worn or weakened back tooth
  • An old crown that needs replacement

Not every situation suits a same-day restoration. Some complex cases, such as long-span bridges, certain front teeth or restorations requiring specialised shading, may be better made by a dental laboratory. For smaller areas of damage, a white filling may be enough. We will explain the most suitable option for your tooth.

What Happens at Your Appointment

Assessment

We examine the tooth, take digital X-rays and check your bite. If a crown is recommended, we explain why, outline alternatives and provide a quote.

Preparation and Scanning

Local anaesthetic is used to keep you comfortable. Any decay or old filling material is removed, and the tooth is shaped to make room for the crown. We then scan the tooth with our 3D intra-oral scanner.

Design and Milling

Your crown is designed digitally to fit your tooth and bite. It is then milled from ceramic in our practice while you relax, which usually takes a short time.

Fitting

We try in the crown, check the fit and bite, then bond it into place and polish it. In many cases, you leave with your final crown on the same day.

Aftercare and Ongoing Care

Your tooth and gums may feel tender for a day or two. Wait until the anaesthetic has worn off before eating. Brush and floss around your crown as you would your natural teeth, and attend regular check-ups and cleans. A crown does not prevent decay at the edge where it meets the tooth, so daily cleaning remains important. If you grind your teeth, a night splint may be recommended.

Why Choose Precision Dental for CEREC Crowns

Our in-house CEREC system and 3D scanner allow us to design and create many restorations on site, which can mean fewer appointments and no temporary crown. Dr Billy Choi has used digital dentistry for many years and takes great care to achieve a precise fit and a comfortable bite.
