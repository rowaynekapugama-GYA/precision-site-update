General Dentistry

Children's Dentistry in Fortitude Valley

A child's early experiences at the dentist can shape how they feel about dental care for years to come. At Precision Dental, we make children's visits relaxed, positive and fun wherever possible, while providing careful preventive care that supports healthy teeth as they grow.

We also partner with parents and carers, sharing practical advice that helps build healthy habits at home.

What Is Children's Dentistry?

Children's dentistry focuses on the oral health needs of infants, children and teenagers. It places a strong emphasis on prevention and education, along with early detection and gentle treatment of concerns such as decay.

Tooth decay remains one of the most common chronic health conditions affecting Australian children, yet it is largely preventable. Regular visits, good home care and a tooth-friendly diet all make a difference.

Is Children's Dentistry Right for Your Child?

We recommend bringing your child for their first dental visit around their first birthday, or when their first teeth appear. Early visits allow children to become familiar with the dental environment before any problems arise.

Regular visits are important for all children, and particularly those who:

  • Have visible white spots, brown marks or holes in their teeth
  • Snack frequently or drink juice or milk from a bottle at night
  • Have had dental decay before
  • Play sport and may need a mouthguard
  • Have teeth that appear crowded or misaligned
  • Feel anxious about dental visits

Families who are eligible for the Child Dental Benefits Schedule are welcome, and our team can help check your child's eligibility.

What Happens at Your Appointment

A Friendly Welcome

We keep things calm and unhurried. Younger children can sit on a parent's lap for their first visit, and we explain each step in simple, reassuring language.

Examination

We gently check your child's teeth, gums and bite, look for early signs of decay and monitor how teeth are developing. X-rays are taken only when needed.

Preventive Care

Depending on your child's needs, we may clean their teeth, apply fluoride varnish to strengthen enamel or place fissure sealants to protect the deep grooves of back teeth.

Tips for Home

We show your child, and you, how to brush effectively, and share advice about diet, drinks and dummy or thumb-sucking habits.

Preparing Your Child for Their Visit

Book morning appointments or times outside nap schedules for younger children. Avoid words such as needle or pain, and try reading a picture book about visiting the dentist together. If your child has additional needs, please let us know beforehand so we can plan the visit around them.

Aftercare and Ongoing Care

Help children brush twice a day with a children's fluoride toothpaste until they can brush well on their own, usually around age eight. Offer water between meals, limit sugary snacks and avoid putting children to bed with a bottle of milk or juice. Book regular check-ups as recommended.

Why Choose Precision Dental for Children's Dentistry

Our team takes a warm, patient approach with children and never rushes a nervous child. A ceiling-mounted television offers a welcome distraction, and our oral health therapist provides preventive care in a relaxed, family-friendly way. We also see children for urgent care, including children's dental emergencies.

Fees, Health Funds and Payment Options

The cost of your treatment depends on your individual needs. After your examination, we talk you through your options and the expected fees before any treatment begins. We accept most major health funds, process claims on the spot through HICAPS and are a preferred provider for Bupa, HCF and CBHS. Interest-free payment plans are available through DentiCare for eligible patients, subject to terms and conditions. You can learn more on our Prices and Payment Options page.
