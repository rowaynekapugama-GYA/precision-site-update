About Us

About Precision Dental

Tucked inside HQ South Tower on Wickham Street, Precision Dental is a welcoming, modern practice in the heart of Fortitude Valley. We look after locals, families and people who work across inner Brisbane, with many patients joining us from neighbouring Newstead, New Farm, Teneriffe, Bowen Hills and Spring Hill.

We have shaped our practice around how people really feel about visiting the dentist. From the moment you arrive, our team is here to make your visit relaxed, unhurried and easy to fit into your week.

Why Choose Us

Why Choose Precision Dental

  • 01/

    We believe you deserve straightforward information about your oral health. We explain what we find, show you images from our intra-oral camera where helpful, outline your options and discuss expected costs before treatment begins. There is never any pressure to proceed.

  • 02/

    Many people feel uneasy about dental visits, and we take that seriously. Our team works gently and at a pace that suits you, and we offer happy gas for patients who would like extra support to relax during treatment.

  • 03/

    Your care is tailored to your individual needs. We invest in dental technology such as digital X-rays, a digital OPG, 3D intra-oral scanning and CEREC to support accurate diagnosis and careful clinical work.

  • 04/

    We are open five days a week, with appointments until 7pm on Tuesdays and from 8am on Fridays. Free on-site parking, on-the-spot health fund claims through HICAPS and interest-free payment plans help make your visit as easy as possible.

  • 05/

    As an accredited practice, we follow rigorous infection prevention and control protocols, with thorough cleaning between patients and sterilisation of reusable instruments.
