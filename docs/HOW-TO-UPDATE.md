# Updating the Precision Dental website

A step-by-step guide for the Precision Dental team. You do not need any technical knowledge.
If you get stuck, contact GYA on 1300 889 840 or hello@generateyouraudience.com.

---

## Before you write anything: the advertising rules

Everything on a dental practice's website is advertising under AHPRA's rules. Before you save:

- **No testimonials or reviews** about clinical care, including quotes from patients.
- **No claims** such as "pain-free", "guaranteed", "best" or "expert", and nothing that pressures people to book quickly. <!-- qa:allow: this rule quotes the words to avoid -->
- **Offers** must state the exact price, what is included and the dates the offer runs (see
  "The new patient offer" below).
- **Posts about surgical or invasive treatment** need the risk statement (a tick box in the post).
- Practitioner names, qualifications and registration details must match the public register.

If you are unsure, save it as a draft and ask GYA to check it.

## The two buttons that matter

- **Save** keeps your change in the dashboard. Nothing changes on the website yet.
- **Publish website** puts every saved change live. It takes about two minutes. You can make
  several changes, then publish once.

The Dashboard tells you when you have **changes waiting to publish**.

## Signing in

1. Go to **precisiondentistry.com.au/admin**.
2. Enter your email and password, then **Sign in**.
3. The first time, you are asked to choose your own password (at least 10 characters with letters
   and numbers).

Forgotten your password? Ask GYA to reset it. You will get a temporary one and choose a new one when
you sign in.

## Change the wording on a page

1. Click **Pages** in the menu on the left.
2. Find the page (use **Find a page** at the top) and click its name.
3. Each part of the page is listed as a section, for example "Page header", "Why Choose Us" or
   "Questions and answers". Click a section to open it.
4. Change the words in the boxes. Headings, paragraphs, list items, button wording and links each
   have their own box.
5. Click **Save changes**, then **Preview** to see the page as it will look.
6. When you are happy, click **Publish website**.

**Words in curly brackets**, like `{phone}` or `{booking-link}`, are filled in automatically from
Site Settings. Leave them as they are. If the phone number changes, change it once in Site Settings,
not on each page.

Some boxes allow bold, italic and links: select the words, then click **B**, **I** or **Link**.

---

## Swap a photo

1. Open the page and the section with the photo.
2. Under the photo, click **Choose from library** to use a photo you have already uploaded, or
   **Upload** to add one from your computer.
3. Fill in **Photo description (alt text)**: describe what is in the photo for someone who cannot
   see it, for example "Dr Billy Choi explaining an X-ray to a patient".
4. **Save changes**, then **Publish website**.

To undo, click **Put back the original photo**.

Photos are resized and compressed automatically, so a photo straight from a phone or camera is fine.
Only use photos the practice owns or has permission to use, and photos of patients only with their
written consent.

---

## Move or hide a section

- **Move:** in the page's list of sections, drag the dotted handle on the left of a section up or
  down. (On a keyboard: Tab to the handle, press Space, use the arrow keys, press Space again.)
- **Hide:** click **Hide** on the section. It stays in the dashboard so you can show it again later.
- The page header always stays at the top.

**Save changes**, check with **Preview**, then **Publish website**.

---

## Write a blog post

1. Click **Blog Posts**, then **Write a new post**.
2. Type the **Title**. The web address is filled in for you.
3. Write the post in the large box. Use the toolbar:
   - **Heading** for each main part, **Subheading** inside it;
   - **B** and **I** for bold and italic, **Link** to link to another page (a page on this website
     starts with `/`, for example `/contact/`);
   - **Bullet list**, **Numbered list** and **Quote**;
   - **Photo** to add a photo from the Media Library;
   - **Book/Call buttons** to add the Book Online and Call buttons.
4. On the right: add a **Featured photo** and its description, pick the **Categories**, and tick the
   **risk statement** box if the post is about a surgical or invasive procedure.
5. Optional: add **Frequently asked questions** under the post, and a short **Summary** for the blog
   list (otherwise the first sentence is used).
6. Fill in the **SEO** section (see below).
7. Click **Publish this post** (or **Save draft** to finish later), then **Publish website**.

A published post appears on the Dental Articles page, in the sitemap and in Google's article
listings after you publish the website.

**To take a post down:** open it and click **Unpublish (make it a draft)**, then **Publish
website**. **To delete it:** click **Delete post**.

## Update opening hours, phone, email, address or the booking link

1. Click **Site Settings**.
2. Change the details in **Practice details**, **Opening hours** or **Booking and payments**.
   - Hours: switch a day to Open or Closed and set the times.
   - The **Online booking link** is where every Book Online button goes.
   - **Preferred provider health funds** is written as it reads in a sentence, for example
     "Bupa, HCF and CBHS".
3. Click **Save settings**, then **Publish website**.

These details update everywhere at once: the header, footer, the Book Your Visit band, the contact
page, every page that mentions them, and the details Google shows for the practice.

Hours written into a page's own wording, for example an answer in the questions on the homepage,
are changed on that page.

## Change what Google shows (SEO)

For one page: open the page, click the **SEO** tab.
For a post: open the post and scroll to **SEO**.
To see every page at once: click **SEO** in the menu, then **Edit** next to a page.

- **Title in Google:** aim for 30 to 60 characters.
- **Description in Google:** aim for 70 to 155 characters.
- The coloured label tells you if it is Good, Short or Too long, and the preview shows roughly how
  it will look in Google.
- **Share image:** the picture shown when the page is shared on Facebook, LinkedIn or in messages.
- Leave **Canonical address** blank unless GYA asks you to fill it in.
- **Hide this page from Google** keeps the page on the website but asks search engines not to list it.

**Save**, then **Publish website**.

## Update the team

1. Click **Team**.
2. Click a person to open them, change their details, then **Save team**.
3. **Add a team member** at the bottom. **Hide** keeps someone off the website without deleting them.
   Drag the dotted handle to change the order.
4. **Publish website**.

Use names, roles and qualifications exactly as they appear on each practitioner's registration.

## Read and follow up enquiries

Every message sent through the website's enquiry form appears under **Enquiries**, as well as being
emailed to reception.

- Click a name to read the message and add a note for the team.
- Tick **Followed up** once someone has replied.
- **Download for Excel (CSV)** gives you every enquiry as a spreadsheet.

The red number next to Enquiries in the menu is how many have not been followed up.

## The Media Library

- **Uploaded photos:** everything uploaded through the dashboard. Click a photo to change its
  description, **Upload a replacement** (the new photo takes its place on every page that uses it),
  or delete it.
- **Website photos:** the photos the website was built with. **Replace everywhere** swaps one for a
  new photo on every page, in the menus and in share images. **Put back** restores the original.

Publish the website after any change.

## The new patient offer

The $229 offer appears in three places: the strip under the homepage hero, the New Patients page and
a pop-up in the middle of the screen, shown once per visit a couple of seconds after the first page opens. All three, and the terms page, come from one place.

1. Click **Site Settings**, then **New patient offer**.
2. Change the price (numbers only), what is included (one item per line), the **Offer period** (the
   dates it runs) or the **Full terms and conditions**.
3. Use the switches to show or hide the homepage strip or the pop-up, or the whole offer.
4. **Save settings**, then **Publish website**.

The rules: the price, what is included and the dates must be clear, and the terms must be easy to
find. No wording that pushes people to book quickly, such as "hurry" or "limited time": the dashboard
will not save it. Do not call anything "free" unless the practice has approved it.

## The price list

People ask for the price list through the form on the homepage, the New Patients page and Prices and
Payment Options. Each request goes to SmileOx (tagged "Price List Download"), to reception, and to
**Enquiries** (choose **Price list downloads** to see just these).

- **The PDF is built in.** The designed October 2026 price list is part of the website. To change a
  fee, ask GYA: we update the Prices and Payment Options page and the PDF together. After someone fills in the form they can
  open the PDF straight away, and SmileOx emails it as well.
- **To send a different PDF instead:** Site Settings > **Price list** > **Upload a different PDF**,
  then **Save settings**. "Use the built-in PDF again" switches back.
- **Who emails it:** normally SmileOx. If SmileOx is not set up yet, choose "The website" and the
  website emails a link to the PDF straight away.
- The consent wording and the thank-you message are in the same panel.

## Before and after photos

Each cosmetic and restorative treatment page has a before and after gallery that stays hidden until it
has a photo set that is ready.

1. Click **Before & After**, then **Add a case**.
2. Choose the **Treatment page** and give it a title, for example "Porcelain veneers, upper front teeth".
3. Add the **Before photo** and **After photo**, with a short description of each.
4. Tick the three checks, only when they are true:
   - written consent to use the photos in advertising is on file;
   - the photos have not been edited or retouched;
   - before and after were taken with the same lighting, angle and distance.
5. Switch on **Show on the website**, **Save cases**, then **Publish website**.

A case that is missing anything says **Not shown** and lists what is still needed. Never use stock
photos or photos of someone who is not a patient of the practice. The risk statement is always shown
with the photos.

## Registration numbers

Each practitioner's AHPRA registration appears in the footer of every page and on their team card.
Click **Team**, open the person, and fill in **Registration type** (for example General Dentist or Oral
Health Therapist) and **AHPRA registration number**, exactly as on the public register. Leave both
blank for non-clinical staff. **Save team**, then **Publish website**.

---

## Health funds and government schemes

The **Health Funds and Government Schemes** page is edited like any other page (Pages): the preferred
provider paragraph, the list of accepted funds, the CDBS section and the DVA line all live there. Three
lines only appear once the practice confirms them, under Site Settings > **Health funds and government
schemes**: on-the-spot claiming with HICAPS (on), bulk billing for the Child Dental Benefits Schedule
and billing DVA directly (both off until confirmed). The CDBS amounts ($1,158 for periods starting in
2026, $1,132 for 2025) change each January: check them with Services Australia and update the page.

The address, the "(Corner of Brookes St)" note and the service area line under it are in Site Settings >
**Practice details**, and change everywhere at once.

---

## Google reviews band

The homepage shows "Over 500 Google Reviews" under the offer, linking to the practice's Google listing.
To update it, click **Site Settings**, then **Google reviews band**:

- **Number of reviews:** round down to what Google shows (for example 500 shows as "Over 500").
- **Star rating:** as Google shows it, for example 4.9. Leave it blank to show the number only.
- **Google Business Profile link** and **Google logo**: GYA usually sets these once.

**Save settings**, then **Publish website**. Review wording is never put on the website: under AHPRA's
rules, patient reviews used in advertising are testimonials. Only the number and the rating are shown.

---

## Good habits

- Preview before you publish.
- Keep sentences short and plain, and use Australian spelling.
- Do not paste text straight from Word with lots of formatting: paste, then tidy it up.
- If something looks wrong after publishing, change it back and publish again, or contact GYA.
