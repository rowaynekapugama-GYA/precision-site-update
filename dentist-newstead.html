Dentist for Newstead

Considered Dental Care That Fits a Busy Week

Precision Dental is a short trip from Newstead, just across the boundary in Fortitude Valley. We help local professionals and residents look after their smiles with care that is thorough, clearly explained and easy to schedule.

Book Online Call (07) 3852 1160

About Precision Dental

Newstead has grown into one of Brisbane's liveliest riverside neighbourhoods, and many of the people who live and work here juggle full calendars. We respect that. Our team keeps you informed about timing before treatment starts, and we plan longer courses of care in stages that suit your commitments.

Our treatment rooms are equipped with digital imaging and a 3D intra-oral scanner, so records can be taken quickly and without traditional impression putty. You can read more About Precision Dental and how we approach every patient.

Our Dental Services

Cosmetic Dentistry

Straightening crowded teeth with Invisalign, brightening your smile with professional teeth whitening or refining tooth shape with veneers are all possible through our Cosmetic Dentistry services, once your teeth and gums are healthy.

Explore Cosmetic Dentistry

Restorative Dentistry

With CEREC in the practice, many cracked or heavily filled teeth can be restored with a ceramic crown in a single visit, saving you a second appointment.

Explore Restorative Dentistry

General Dentistry

A regular check-up and clean keeps small issues small. We also treat gum inflammation, sensitive teeth and wisdom teeth concerns.

Explore General Dentistry

Emergency Dentistry

When something breaks or starts to ache, our Emergency Dentistry team will do our best to see you promptly, often on the same day.

Explore Emergency Dentistry

Why Newstead Patients Choose Precision Dental

Honesty

Cosmetic treatment should never be sold. We explain the benefits, limitations and costs of each option so you can decide in your own time.

Comfort

Noise-cancelling headphones and a relaxed pace make longer appointments more comfortable, and we pause whenever you need a break.

Quality

Digital planning, including Digital Smile Design where appropriate, helps us map out cosmetic and restorative care with precision.

Convenience

Early starts on Fridays, late finishes on Tuesdays and same-day crowns in many cases help you spend less time away from your desk.

Safety

Our infection prevention and control procedures are regularly reviewed as part of our practice accreditation.
